What to look for

  • Ask for work that went through MLR or compliance review.
  • Check for 3D medical or device animation if you sell a product.
  • Look for HIPAA-aware filming of patients and clinical staff.

How this list is ranked

Every studio here passed the same three checks as the national ranking — US headquarters, at least 60% corporate work, at least three client works in the last 12 months — and carries the same score. Studios appear on this page when their own site lists this industry among its main sectors.
