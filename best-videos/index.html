Methodology

How we score

Each video gets up to 100 points. Every fact comes from a public source we can link to: the studio’s case page, the client’s site, a Clutch review or an awards database.

20

Freshness

Made or published in 2025–2026: 20 points; 2024: 12; 2023: 5. Videos older than 2023 are not eligible.

20

Client

Client with revenue above $1 billion (or a large health system or government agency): 20; a well-known mid-market or venture-backed company: 10; a small business or unnamed client: 5.

15

Client testimonial

A positive quote from the client about this project, published by the studio or on Clutch: 15 for a detailed quote (two sentences or more), 8 for a short one.

15

Published result

An outcome the studio or client published for this video: 15 if it includes a number (sales, sign-ups, search growth, money raised), 6 if it is described in words only. Where the video ran, view counts and the number of deliverables do not count.

10

Repeat client

The client hired the studio again for a separate project.

10

Awards

Awards won by this video or its campaign since 2023: Cannes Lions, Clio, D&AD or One Show 10; Emmy, Webby, Muse or ADDY 7; Telly, Davey, Communicator or AVA 4; other awards and shortlists 2.

10

Studio score

The studio’s score in our 2026 ranking, scaled to 10 points for the highest-scoring studio.

Rules

  • Only real client work by US studios that qualified for our 2026 ranking. Spec ads, reels and a studio’s own promos are excluded, and so are videos where the studio only shot or edited.
  • One video per studio per list, and one video appears in no more than two lists.
  • Length limits: up to 4 minutes for explainers, 5 minutes for brand videos and animation, 10 minutes for B2B and healthcare.
  • Ties are broken by the published result, then by recency.
  • Vertical social cuts (Shorts, 9:16) are not ranked; videos must play on this site.
  • Views and likes are not used: they mostly reflect ad spend, not quality.
  • The selection is automatic. No studio pays to be included and no one hand-picks the order. Lists are refreshed every six months.

The candidate pool comes from the portfolios of the 107 studios that qualified for our 2026 ranking. Spotted an error or a missing award? Tell us and we will re-check it.
