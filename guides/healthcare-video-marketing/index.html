Before you brief

What to plan for

Plan for medical, legal and regulatory review

Pharma and MedTech scripts usually go through medical, legal and regulatory (MLR) review before production and again before release. Build those rounds into the schedule and ask the studio how many review cycles its price includes.

Consent for every patient on camera

Anyone filmed in a clinical setting needs a signed release, and patient information must stay off screen. Ask how the studio handles releases and HIPAA-sensitive locations.

Claims must match the label

For drugs and devices, on-screen claims have to match approved labelling and include fair balance where required. Agree who signs off on claims before animation starts.

Accessible by default

Captions, audio description where needed and readable on-screen text matter more in healthcare, where viewers may be older or unwell.

Brief checklist

  • Audience: patients, caregivers, clinicians or payers
  • One action you want them to take
  • Who reviews the script (MLR, legal, clinical lead) and how many rounds
  • Whether real patients or staff appear, and who collects releases
  • Approved claims, references and safety information to include
  • Where it will run: website, waiting room, sales reps, social
  • Budget range and deadline
