Before you brief

What to plan for

Long buying cycles need many assets

B2B deals involve several decision makers over months. One shoot planned well can serve each stage: a brand film, an explainer, a demo and customer stories.

Plant and site access

Filming on a factory floor or job site means safety briefings, PPE, restricted areas and production schedules. Agree access and confidentiality before the shoot day.

3D where cameras cannot go

Machinery internals, processes and products that do not exist yet are clearer in 3D animation built from CAD files.

Sales enablement first

Ask the sales team which objections they hear most. The best B2B videos answer those directly.

Brief checklist

  • Funnel stage and buying role the video is for
  • The one action you want next
  • Site access, safety rules and confidential areas
  • CAD files or product renders that can be reused
  • Customer who could appear in a story video
  • Where it will run: website, trade shows, sales decks, LinkedIn
  • Budget range and deadline
