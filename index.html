Methodology

How every score is built

Three checks decide who qualifies — a US headquarters, at least 60% corporate work and at least three client works in the last 12 months. Twelve weighted criteria and an award multiplier decide the order.

Projects for companies with revenue above $1 billion (Fortune 1000 / Global 2000 level) that are dated within the last 24 months on the studio's site, YouTube or Vimeo. Six or more earn the maximum. Government bodies, universities and nonprofits are not counted here.

$1B+ companies shown as clients anywhere on the studio's site, regardless of date. Ten or more earn the maximum. Weighted lower than recent work because logos can be years old.

Distinct client works published in the last 12 months across the site, YouTube and Vimeo. The studio's own reels, podcasts, promos and bulk re-uploads of old work are not counted. 24+ earns the maximum; fewer than 3 removes a studio from the ranking.

Published outcomes — sales, conversions, views, ROAS, funds raised. Five or more figures earn the maximum; case studies without numbers earn partial credit.

Google Business Profile and Clutch (plus Trustpilot where reachable), combined. A Bayesian average stops a handful of perfect reviews from outranking a long record; volume and presence on two or more platforms add points.

Founders named on the site, with an industry background (director, producer, DP, editor, animator, agency creative, journalist) and public thought leadership: articles, a video blog or podcast, talks, press quotes. Co-founders count too.

Years in business. Twenty or more earn the maximum.

A portfolio, case studies and client testimonials on the site — a third of the points each.

Industries served (five or more) and range of video types (five or more).

Published prices or ranges earn full points; budget ranges in a quote form earn partial points.

AI-assisted production, virtual production, 3D/VFX or localization, verified in client work.

How many of our six source lists the studio appeared in. Kept low so marketing spend cannot buy a rank.
