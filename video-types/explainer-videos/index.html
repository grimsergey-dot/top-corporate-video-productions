What to look for

  • Ask to see the script process — most explainers fail at the script, not the animation.
  • Compare published prices: explainers range from about $3,500 to $30,000+.
  • Check for localization if you sell in several markets.

How this list is ranked

Every studio here passed the same three checks as the national ranking — US headquarters, at least 60% corporate work, at least three client works in the last 12 months — and carries the same score. Studios appear on this page when their own site shows work in this category.
