Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

Top Corporate Video Productions (topcorporatevideoproduction.com, “we”, “us”) is a ranking of US corporate video production companies, published by Lava Media, which is itself ranked in it. Lava Media is the controller of the personal data described here. To contact us about privacy, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”.

What we collect and why

1. When you send a brief to studios

We collect your name, company, work email, the message and optional details you write (budget, type of video), the studios you select and the page you sent it from. We use them to send your brief to the studios you chose, after you confirm it by email. The legal basis is your request (taking steps at your request) and your consent when you confirm.

  • Who receives it: only the studios you tick, plus a copy to Lava Media as the publisher of this ranking. Each studio handles your brief under its own privacy policy and replies to you directly.
  • How long we keep it: an unconfirmed brief is deleted automatically after 48 hours. A confirmed brief is deleted from our form system as soon as it has been sent; copies remain in the recipients’ and our email inboxes and in our email provider’s delivery logs.
  • Abuse protection: to stop spam we check the request with Cloudflare Turnstile and keep rate-limit counters for up to about 25 hours: a one-way hash of your IP address and, separately, your email address.

2. When you use the contact and feedback form

We collect your name, email, topic and message to answer you, correct facts or consider a nomination. The form is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you and keeping the ranking accurate. We keep these messages while they are needed for that purpose.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, approximate location, device and events such as video plays, clicks to studio websites and opened brief forms. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes your IP address. Where the law requires it, we rely on your consent; you can also block analytics with your browser settings or Google’s opt-out add-on.

4. Embedded videos, fonts and hosting

  • Videos play from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) or Vimeo only after you click a video; preview images load from their image servers. Those services may then process your data under their own policies.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages; our brief service runs on Cloudflare; emails are sent through Resend. These providers process technical data, such as IP addresses, to deliver the service.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and studios cannot buy access to your data or to a better position in the ranking.

Studios in the ranking

We list studios using business information they publish themselves: company name, website, services, public work, published prices and public reviews. Studios receive briefs only when a visitor chooses them and confirms. Every brief email contains a link to stop receiving briefs. To correct a fact about your studio, use the feedback form with a link to the public source.

International transfers

Our service providers are based in, or process data in, the United States. Where data from the EU or UK is transferred, we rely on the safeguards our providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent at any time. California residents can also ask what personal information we collected and request its deletion; we do not sell or share it as defined by California law. Send a request through the contact form (topic “Privacy request”). You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the new date at the top.
