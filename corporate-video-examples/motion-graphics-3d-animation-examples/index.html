What works

What makes business animation work

Design before motion

The best examples agree style frames before animating. Changing a look after animation starts is the most expensive kind of revision.

Use 3D where cameras cannot go

Inside a machine, a device or the human body — that is where 3D earns its higher cost. For abstract ideas, 2D is usually enough.

Motion with a purpose

Every movement should lead the eye to the next point. Decorative motion makes data harder to read.

Reuse your assets

CAD files, product renders and brand illustrations cut modelling and design time.
