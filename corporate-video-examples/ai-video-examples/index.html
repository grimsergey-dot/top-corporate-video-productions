What works

What makes AI video work for brands

Human direction

The strongest AI examples still have a human script, art direction and edit. AI replaces the shoot, not the idea.

Consent and rights

Avatar and voice-clone videos need the person’s consent and clear usage rights. Ask the studio how it handles both.

Use it for variants

AI is cheapest where you need many versions: languages, audiences, formats.

Disclose when it matters

Some platforms and audiences expect AI-generated content to be labelled.
