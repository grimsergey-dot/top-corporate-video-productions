What works

What makes a corporate video work

One audience, one action

The strongest examples speak to one viewer — a buyer, a patient, a new hire — and ask for one thing. A video for everyone usually works for no one.

Show, do not list

Good corporate videos show the product in use, the plant floor or the customer’s result instead of reading out features and values.

Real people over scripts

Customers, engineers and clinicians talking in their own words carry more trust than polished narration. Most testimonial and recruiting examples here use real staff.

Plan the cut-downs

One shoot can give a hero film, 15-second social edits and a vertical version. Planning them up front costs far less than re-editing later.
