What works

What the best explainer videos have in common

The problem comes first

Strong explainers open with the viewer’s problem in the first few seconds, then show how the product solves it. Feature lists come last, if at all.

One idea per scene

Each scene carries one message. When a scene tries to say two things, viewers remember neither.

Short and focused

Explainers usually run one to two minutes. When the story needs more, a short series works better than one long video.

A style that fits the brand

Kinetic type and icons suit data-heavy products; character animation suits consumer and patient stories; live action suits physical products and services.
