Cost drivers

What drives the cost of a commercial or brand film

Shoot days and locations

Every shoot day adds crew, gear and location fees, and every company move between locations eats into the day. Multi-location, multi-day spots sit at the top of the range.

Crew and director

A small documentary-style crew costs far less than a full commercial crew with a director, DP, gaffer, art department and producers.

Talent and usage rights

Actors, voice talent and music are paid by usage: a national TV buy costs more than organic social. Union talent adds session and residual fees.

Art direction and post

Sets, props, wardrobe, colour grading, VFX and sound mix are separate lines. Heavy VFX or animation inside a live-action spot adds post-production weeks.

Versions

A hero spot plus 15- and 6-second cut-downs, vertical versions and alternate end-cards multiply editing time.
