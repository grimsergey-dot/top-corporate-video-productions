Cost drivers

What drives the cost of an explainer video

Length

Explainers are priced by finished length. Some studios bill per minute of narration; a 60–90 second piece is the usual brief, and every extra 30 seconds adds script, design and animation time.

Animation style

Kinetic text and icon-based motion graphics sit at the bottom of the range. Custom illustrated characters, frame-by-frame animation and 3D push the price up because every scene is drawn or modelled from scratch.

Script and storyboard

A studio that writes the script and storyboard with you charges for that strategy work. If you bring an approved script, the price drops.

Voice-over, music and sound

Professional voice talent, licensed music and custom sound design are line items. Extra languages mean extra voice sessions and re-timed animation.

Revisions and versions

Most packages include a fixed number of revision rounds. Cut-downs for ads and social, aspect-ratio versions and localisation are billed on top.
