Cost drivers

What drives the cost of an animated or 3D video

2D or 3D

2D motion graphics reuse shapes, type and icons; 3D needs models, textures, lighting and render time for every shot. Photoreal 3D product or medical animation is the most expensive kind of animation to produce.

Length and complexity

Animation is priced by finished seconds and by how much moves on screen. A short loop for a product page costs a fraction of a 90-second story with characters, scene changes and camera moves.

Assets you already have

CAD files, brand illustrations and existing 3D models cut modelling and design time. Starting from nothing means the studio builds every asset.

Style frames and revisions

Good studios agree style frames before animating. Changes after animation starts are the most expensive kind of revision, so the number of included rounds matters.

Deliverables

Extra aspect ratios, cut-downs, transparent-background renders for web and localised versions are usually priced separately.
