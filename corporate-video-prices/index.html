What drives the price

Length, shoot days, locations, on-camera talent, animation complexity and the number of cut-downs. A one-day interview-led corporate video and a multi-location brand film can differ by ten times, which is why we show the actual video next to each price.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The median exact price for a corporate video among studios in our ranking is $11.5k, and the middle half of prices falls between $7k and $30k. Price depends mostly on shoot days, locations, on-camera talent and how many versions you need.

The median exact price for an animated explainer among studios in our ranking is $10k, and the middle half of prices falls between $5k and $20k. Length, illustration style and the amount of custom animation drive the difference.

The median exact price for a brand film or commercial among studios in our ranking is $14.5k, and the middle half of prices falls between $6.3k and $30k. Multi-day shoots with crews, casting and locations sit at the top of the range.

The median exact price for an AI-generated video among studios in our ranking is $3.4k, and the middle half of prices falls between $1.2k and $7.2k. AI production removes most shoot costs, but scripting, art direction and editing are still billed.

A “starting from” price or a budget band like $10,000–$50,000 tells you little about a specific video. We only show prices a studio states for a specific video, package or project, with a spread of no more than 30%, so you can see exactly what that money bought.
